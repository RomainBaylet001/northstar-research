## Why these moves occurred (do not infer automatic alpha)

In September 2026, **FTSE Russell reclassified Greece to Developed** and **Vietnam to Secondary Emerging**, effective 21 September. This helps explain many Greek companies leaving VFEA and Vietnamese companies joining. It does not mean those Greek companies deteriorated fundamentally or newly included Vietnamese companies will outperform. [FTSE Russell announcement](https://www.lseg.com/en/media-centre/press-releases/ftse-russell/2026/ftse-country-classification-review-september-2026).

VWCE's additions include AI infrastructure, optical networks, semiconductors, power equipment and diversified industrial companies; these are candidates **for independent fundamental research**, not investment recommendations.

Before investing in any new ETF constituent, screen its actual revenue growth, profits/free cash flow, balance sheet, valuation, liquidity, ownership and price movement **before and after** the index/ETF disclosure. A passive ETF holding addition is not itself evidence of an informed institutional stock-selection bet.

**No July membership comparison is presented**: July's full ISIN-level archived basket has not been obtained. Don't infer full three-month additions from the August→September list alone.
